# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the brief: maintainable TypeScript with a 3D renderer and real rigid-body physics. React, Vite, Three.js, Rapier.

## Users

People experimenting with miniature marble machines on desktop and mobile browsers.

## Product Purpose

Build, run, save, and photograph modular 3D marble tracks with actual simulated physics.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Eleven modules, three runnable presets, explicit connector snapping, reversible editing, real 3D collisions, local browser saving, validated JSON import/export, PNG photography. No account or paid API needed for the core.

## Brand Commitments

Premium physical toy on a warm ivory tabletop. Coral, cobalt, butter yellow, and mint plastic; metal supports; glossy marbles. The scene dominates compact controls.

## Product Principles

Immediate play. Accurate connections. Honest physical outcomes. Recoverable editing. Clear compact controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard editing, labeled controls, visible focus, reduced motion, touch controls, readable contrast.

## Execution

The user explicitly asks to resolve routine choices independently and only ask blocking questions. Their detailed brief supplies product and visual direction; no additional interview is needed.
